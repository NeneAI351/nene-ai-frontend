This policy describes the NENE AI app and website. Use our privacy contact form for questions, access or correction requests, or help with deletion. The form works without installing the app or signing in.

Information we process

  • Account information: sign-in email, account identifier, authentication/session information, optional display name, profile photo, brand preferences and account-setup agreement records. Authentication is handled by Supabase; NENE AI's app database does not store your plaintext password.
  • Creative content: prompts, scripts, character descriptions and reference images, uploaded photographs and recordings, generated voice tracks, videos, project details, job status and relevant settings. Upload only content you have rights to use. Avoid unnecessary sensitive personal information.
  • Purchases: credit packages, balances, reservations, payment references, amounts, currency and payment status. Card details are entered with the payment processor, not in NENE AI prompts or feedback.
  • Support and security: reports, privacy enquiries, contact email and messages, request references and technical information needed for authentication, abuse prevention and troubleshooting. Hosting and delivery services may process IP addresses and request metadata.

How we use information

We use this information to authenticate accounts, run creative features, deliver and store your results, manage purchased credits, verify payments, prevent misuse, investigate problems, respond to privacy requests and meet applicable obligations. Profile photos and uploads are optional unless the feature you choose needs them. An uploaded voice recording supplies the speech for audio-driven animation; a typed script is sent to the speech provider when you request paid voice creation.

Services that process information

  • Supabase: authentication and configured database/private storage.
  • Render: backend hosting and configured background services.
  • GitHub Pages and jsDelivr: website and application-library delivery.
  • LTX: video generation, including images, prompts and approved speech required by the selected workflow.
  • Magic Hour: script and selected preset for voice generation, and any separately enabled compatible workflow.
  • OpenAI: script planning/AI Director requests when you choose those features. Planning requests are configured with response storage disabled; provider security and legal retention may still apply.
  • Pixazo: only if a compatible feature is enabled and routes your requested generation there. Its durable video adapter is currently disabled.
  • Paystack or an applicable app-store payment service: checkout, transaction verification, fraud prevention and payment records when enabled.

Only send content for processing when you choose the relevant feature. Provider processing can occur outside your country and is subject to the provider's terms and retention practices. NENE AI does not sell your personal data or add advertising trackers to this interface. This does not mean hosting, authentication or delivery providers receive no technical information.

Security and private delivery

The website and app API use HTTPS. Protected requests are checked against the signed-in account. Source images, recordings and finished media are stored in the configured private bucket; authorized short-lived viewing links are issued when needed. Anyone holding a valid viewing link can access that file until the link expires, so avoid sharing it unintentionally. Files you download or publish are under your control. No service can promise absolute security.

Browser storage

This browser stores sign-in sessions, account-scoped drafts, job references and application files for offline loading. Clearing browser data does not delete the server account. Sign out on shared devices. When a deletion request is confirmed, NENE AI clears that account's local workspace keys and signs out this browser. Other devices and downloaded files must be cleared separately. Privacy Center's local-profile download and clear-drafts controls refer only to browser copies.

Retention

  • Account and creative records remain while your account is active unless you remove them or request account deletion.
  • On account deletion, NENE AI removes the sign-in identity, app profile, photos, characters, prompts, scripts, projects, voice tracks, source files and finished videos. Active work is stopped before cleanup. NENE AI does not silently preserve generation prompts by detaching the account identifier.
  • Minimal accounting records may be kept for up to seven years after account deletion for accounting, disputes, fraud prevention and applicable obligations: payment reference, processor, test/live mode, amount, currency, transaction identifier, status and dates. The deletion archive excludes your profile, email, prompts, uploaded content, authorization URL and card details.
  • A one-way digest of the deleted account's random identifier is retained indefinitely as a security marker to stop old login tokens from recreating the account. It does not contain your email, profile or creative content.
  • Deletion receipts contain processing status and dates and are kept for up to one year after completion, or longer while provider follow-up remains unresolved. The original account identifier and storage manifest are cleared after cleanup. Provider job identifiers are retained while an erasure follow-up is unresolved; completed follow-up records are removed within 90 days of completion.
  • Privacy enquiries are retained while being handled and for up to 90 days after closure. Accounting archives are removed when their retention period expires. Service logs, backups and provider copies can have separate retention; they are not available as active app content after deletion. If a backup is restored, deletion markers must be reapplied before accounts are served.

Delete your NENE AI account and data

Delete account & data

In the app, open Profile → Privacy & Safety → Delete account & data. You can also use the button above in any browser, without reinstalling the app. Sign in to identify your own account, read the consequences and confirm deletion. You do not need to accept new service terms to request deletion. If you cannot sign in, submit an account-deletion access-help request; ownership must be verified before an account is erased.

Deletion is permanent. Download anything you want to keep before confirming. Account access and unused credits are lost; mandatory payment and consumer rights are not waived. Deletion does not by itself cancel a subscription managed by an external store or payment provider. Cancel any such subscription with that provider too.

We issue a receipt and block app access when the request is accepted. Cleanup starts after a short safety delay and normally finishes within 24 hours. Storage, identity-provider or service failures can delay completion and are retried; the receipt shows progress. NENE AI requests provider erasure where supported and records further follow-up when an automatic provider deletion route is unavailable. Provider copies may take longer; a completed local deletion does not assert that every provider's backup or legally retained security record has been erased.

Your choices and requests

In Profile → Privacy Center, use “Download account records” for a private JSON copy of server-held account records. Video, audio and source files, browser-only drafts, security logs and provider-held copies are separate; use Privacy help for additional data requests. Large exports are referred to Privacy help rather than silently truncated. You may review or correct your profile, avoid optional uploads, download results, clear local drafts, request access or deletion through Privacy help, and delete your account. Submitted enquiries receive a reference; receipt means stored, not already resolved. We may need to verify ownership before disclosing or erasing personal information. Only necessary verification information should be supplied.

Children and content rights

NENE AI is not designed for children under 13. Do not upload another person's private information or likeness without appropriate rights and consent. If you believe a child's information or another person's data has been submitted improperly, use Privacy help or the app's abuse-reporting option.

Policy updates

We publish changes on this page with a new effective date. Material changes may also be announced in the app. This policy describes implemented data flows; it is not a statement that Google Play has approved NENE AI.
