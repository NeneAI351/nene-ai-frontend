Privacy Notice

Testing-stage notice · 23 October 2026

Account and service data

NENE AI uses Supabase for account authentication and database/storage services, Render for the backend, and GitHub Pages for this web interface. Account identifiers, account-setup agreement records, payment records, credit balances and generation job records are used to operate and secure the service. Do not put passwords, API keys or payment-card details in prompts or feedback.

Creative content

Prompts and uploaded assets submitted for generation are processed by the configured generation service and provider. Private output access requires server authorization. Downloaded files are under your control; protect them before sharing. Paid generation remains unavailable until the provider, worker and payment setup are verified.

Browser storage

The app stores authentication session information, pending-job references and some workspace state in your browser. Profile display names, brand briefs and feedback/report drafts are stored locally under the signed-in account identifier. Profile drafts are not synchronized to another device and are not sent to support. Privacy Center lets you download or clear those profile drafts. Clearing browser data may remove local work. Sign out on shared devices.

Payments

Purchases are currently closed. Payment verification and credit records must be handled by the backend; a browser balance does not prove payment. When enabled, payment-provider processing and its privacy terms also apply.

Retention and deletion

Clearing local profile drafts does not delete backend records, videos or your account. A verified account-deletion process, published retention schedule and operational privacy contact are not yet available. These are launch blockers; do not treat this testing-stage notice as confirmation that those processes are complete. Do not upload sensitive personal content during testing.

Your choices

You can sign out, avoid submitting optional content, download local profile drafts and clear those drafts through Privacy Center. There is currently no functional interface for changing server retention or requesting account deletion.
